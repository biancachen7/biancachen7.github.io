About

Data is a compass, not just a record

My first dataset was my own. Running a small online bakery in college, I tracked orders, repeat customers, and how changes to my listings shifted demand — and learned that data isn't just a record of what happened, it's a compass for what to do next.

Since then I've pressure-tested that instinct in real product and business environments: decomposing engagement and churn drivers from app behavior data at Meitu, tying e-commerce traffic and campaign metrics to conversion at CHARLES & KEITH, and building the inventory analytics that guide restocking decisions across Daiso stores.

What ties it together: I don't stop at a cleaner chart. I dig for the better question — is this a visibility problem or a loyalty problem? — and translate messy, cross-platform data into a narrative that product, marketing, and operations teams can act on.
